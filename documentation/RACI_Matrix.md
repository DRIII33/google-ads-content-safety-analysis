# Cross-Functional RACI Matrix: Content Safety & Abuse Enforcement

| Operational Function | Analyst, Content Safety | Product Manager (PM) | Engineering (Eng) | Global Sales (GSO) | Legal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Abuse Trend Detection & Modeling** | **R / A** | C | C | I | I |
| **Enforcement Policy Definition** | C | **A** | C | C | **R** |
| **Automated System Implementation** | C | C | **R / A** | I | I |
| **Advertiser Appeal & Friction Management** | C | I | I | **R / A** | C |
| **Subnet Block & Account Suspension** | **R** | I | **A** | I | C |

* **R = Responsible:** The role that performs the activity.
* **A = Accountable:** The role with final approval and fiduciary ownership.
* **C = Consulted:** The role providing advisory and functional input.
* **I = Informed:** The role notified of outcomes and status changes.
