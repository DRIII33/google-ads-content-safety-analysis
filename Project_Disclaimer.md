## Data Methodology & Synthetic Generation
To ensure strict compliance with privacy standards, proprietary protections, and ethical data practices:
* **Synthetic Data Generation:** All datasets utilized within this project (including `google_ads_q3_traffic.csv` and BigQuery tables) were generated synthetically using Python libraries (`pandas`, `numpy`, `scipy`). No real user data, proprietary advertiser information, or confidential network logs were collected, accessed, or analyzed.
* **Public Information & Scholarly Research:** System designs, threat modeling vectors, regulatory references (e.g., EU AI Act Article 50), and operational workflows were synthesized strictly from publicly available research papers, academic literature, official public documentation, and standard industry threat frameworks.
* **Simulated Environment:** All SQL schemas, statistical Z-Score models, and escalation workflows model the *type* of analytical problems and dataset structures encountered in large-scale Trust & Safety operations, serving purely as a theoretical proof-of-concept.
